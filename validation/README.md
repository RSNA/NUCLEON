## 1. Systematic daughter comparison

**Method.** [`scripts/compare_daughters.py`](../scripts/compare_daughters.py) compares every NUCLEON branch that has a `:decayProduct` with a reference that names daughters explicitly:

- **ICRP Publication 107** (1,252 radionuclides), via the `radioactivedecay` 0.6.1 package (dataset `icrp107_ame2020_nubase2020`), for every parent it covers. 1,250 of the 1,252 are in NUCLEON; Nb-99m and Pm-137m are not.
- **ENSDF decay datasets**, via the `paceENSDF` 0.6.3 package (3,254 datasets), for parents not in ICRP-107. The package holds &alpha;, &beta;&minus; and EC/&beta;+ datasets only.

Links are compared by daughter nucleus (Z, A), since NUCLEON resolves every daughter to its ground state. ICRP-107's isomer labels are matched to NUCLEON's by half-life (e.g. ICRP "Hf-178m" is NUCLEON Hf-178m2, 31 a). ENSDF parent levels are matched to NUCLEON isomers by energy order where the counts agree, and pooled otherwise.

ICRP-107 is fully independent of NUCLEON's source. The IAEA LiveChart draws in part on ENSDF, so ENSDF agreement mainly tests the derivation code, not the source data.

**Results** for the 5,629 links with a daughter:

| Reference | Agree | Agree; reference daughter is an isomer | Branch not in reference | Disagree | Not checkable |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| ICRP-107 | 1,387 | 117 | 71 | 0 | &mdash; |
| ENSDF | 1,752 | &mdash; | 176 | 0 | 670 (mode not in ENSDF package) |
| Neither | &mdash; | &mdash; | &mdash; | &mdash; | 1,456 (parent in neither reference) |

- *Agree; reference daughter is an isomer*: e.g. Mo-99 &rarr; Tc-99m in ICRP-107, Tc-99 in NUCLEON. This follows NUCLEON's documented ground-state convention.
- *Branch not in reference, ICRP-107*: 60 of 71 are branches of &le;1% that ICRP-107 omits (e.g. the C-14 cluster branches of Ra-223 and Ac-225). The rest are newer evaluations (Mn-58m IT 20%, At-219 &beta;&minus; 6.4%, U-228 EC 5%, and others).
- *Branch not in reference, ENSDF*: mostly EC/&beta;+ branches of proton-rich nuclides for which the package has only an &alpha; dataset.
- *Not checkable*: delayed-particle, proton, isomeric-transition and double-&beta; branches, which the ENSDF package does not cover, and exotic nuclides with no evaluated decay dataset.
- DICOM-coded nuclides: 96 of 101 links agree; the other 5 are branches of &le;0.1% that ICRP-107 omits.

29 reference pairs have no NUCLEON counterpart ([`daughter_comparison_reference_only.csv`](daughter_comparison_reference_only.csv)). They are all ENSDF pairs, mostly isomers whose ENSDF levels could not be matched one-to-one, plus small &alpha; branches (I-112, Cs-114).

**Branching ratios vs ICRP-107** ([`branching_vs_icrp107.csv`](branching_vs_icrp107.csv)): 1,575 of 1,595 mode-family comparisons agree within 1 percentage point. The 20 differences are on 11 nuclides with newer evaluations: V-50, Mn-58m, Y-91m, Nb-91m, In-112, Ir-190m2, At-219, U-228, Pu-232, Es-250 and Es-254m.

## 2. NUCLEON vs its IAEA source export

[`scripts/compare_to_source.py`](../scripts/compare_to_source.py) checks every row of [`source-data/IAEA nuclide livechart.csv`](../source-data) against the ontology ([`source_comparison.csv`](source_comparison.csv)).

| Field | Discrepancies | Interpretation |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Decay modes and branching ratios | 3 + 1 | Match, apart from K-40 (NUCLEON EC, source EC+&beta;+), the documented Hf-183/Mn-60 IT relocations, and U-238 SF rounding |
| Half-life in seconds | 833 differ, 49 absent | **Not from this export** (396 differ by &gt;5%) |
| Half-life value and unit as recorded | 865 | Same cause |
| Natural abundance | 287 | **Absent** from NUCLEON for every naturally occurring nuclide |
| Decay width | 14 | Older values, as for half-lives |
| Stability flag | 9 | Bi-209, W-180, Eu-151 and others: see the main README |
| Missing nuclides | 3 | n-1, n-4, n-6 (the neutron is not an element; expected) |

## Findings requiring action

1. **Half-lives come from the earlier build, not the IAEA export.** Decay modes match the export; half-lives match an older dataset (e.g. C-14: 5730 a in NUCLEON, 5700 a in the export). The manuscript states that the IAEA LiveChart supplied half-lives. Either reload `:halfLife`, `:halfLifeValue`, `:halfLifeUnit` and the decay widths from the export, or correct the Methods.
2. **Na-24 half-life unit error.** Recorded as 14.959 **s**; the true value is 14.959 **h** (53,852 s in ICRP-107, 53,842 s in the export). Na-24 is a clinical tracer.
3. **Ground state and isomer swapped** relative to both the export and ICRP-107: Np-242 / Np-242m (330 s vs 132 s). Pr-134 / Pr-134m also differ from ICRP-107, but there NUCLEON matches the export, so it reflects a newer evaluation.
4. **Natural abundance absent** for all 287 naturally occurring nuclides, although the manuscript lists abundance among the imported fields.
5. **Two ICRP-107 isomers not in NUCLEON:** Nb-99m (2.6 min) and Pm-137m (2.4 min).

Items 1&ndash;4 are corrected by reloading these fields from the export. The derived daughters do not depend on them.

## 3. Manual audit

[`scripts/draw_audit_sample.py`](../scripts/draw_audit_sample.py) assigns every nuclide to one stratum and draws a seeded random sample (seed 20261021):

| Stratum | Sampled | Pool |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| DICOM-coded radionuclides | 20 | 87 |
| Metastable isomers | 15 | 709 |
| Flagged stable | 10 | 249 |
| Z &ge; 89 | 10 | 466 |
| Half-life given as a limit or approximation | 5 | 132 |
| Half-life &lt; 1 s or given as a decay width | 10 | 742 |
| Two or more decay branches | 15 | 545 |
| One branch, half-life &ge; 1 s | 15 | 1,150 |
| **Total** | **100** | 4,080 |

**Protocol.** Two reviewers independently fill in the blank columns of [`audit_sample.csv`](audit_sample.csv) against the [IAEA LiveChart of Nuclides](https://nds.iaea.org/livechart/), each in their own copy. For each nuclide they record the IAEA half-life and decay modes, and mark half-life, modes and ratios, daughters, and abundance as Y or N. Discrepancies between reviewers are resolved by discussion. [`audit_sample_key.csv`](audit_sample_key.csv) lists which sampled nuclides the automated comparisons already flagged (42 of 100). Keep it from reviewers until both have finished, so the audit stays blind; then report agreement with and without the key.

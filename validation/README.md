## 1. Systematic daughter comparison

**Method.** [`scripts/compare_daughters.py`](../scripts/compare_daughters.py) compares every NUCLEON branch that has a `:decayProduct` with a reference that names daughters explicitly:

- **ICRP Publication 107** (1,252 radionuclides), via the `radioactivedecay` 0.6.1 package (dataset `icrp107_ame2020_nubase2020`), for every parent it covers. 1,250 of the 1,252 are in NUCLEON; Nb-99m and Pm-137m are not.
- **ENSDF decay datasets**, via the `paceENSDF` 0.6.3 package (3,254 datasets), for parents not in ICRP-107. The package holds &alpha;, &beta;&minus; and EC/&beta;+ datasets only.

Links are compared by daughter nucleus (Z, A), since NUCLEON resolves every daughter to its ground state. ICRP-107's isomer labels are matched to NUCLEON's by half-life (e.g. ICRP "Hf-178m" is NUCLEON Hf-178m2, 31 a). ENSDF parent levels are matched to NUCLEON isomers by energy order where the counts agree, and pooled otherwise.

ICRP-107 is fully independent of NUCLEON's source. The IAEA LiveChart draws in part on ENSDF, so ENSDF agreement mainly tests the derivation code, not the source data.

**Results** for the 5,629 links with a daughter:

| Reference | Agree | Agree; reference daughter is an isomer | Branch not in reference | Disagree | Not checkable |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| ICRP-107 | 1,387 | 117 | 71 | 0 | &mdash; |
| ENSDF | 1,752 | &mdash; | 176 | 0 | 670 (mode not in ENSDF package) |
| Neither | &mdash; | &mdash; | &mdash; | &mdash; | 1,456 (parent in neither reference) |

- *Agree; reference daughter is an isomer*: e.g. Mo-99 &rarr; Tc-99m in ICRP-107, Tc-99 in NUCLEON. This follows NUCLEON's documented ground-state convention.
- *Branch not in reference, ICRP-107*: 60 of 71 are branches of &le;1% that ICRP-107 omits (e.g. the C-14 cluster branches of Ra-223 and Ac-225). The rest are newer evaluations (Mn-58m IT 20%, At-219 &beta;&minus; 6.4%, U-228 EC 5%, and others).
- *Branch not in reference, ENSDF*: mostly EC/&beta;+ branches of proton-rich nuclides for which the package has only an &alpha; dataset.
- *Not checkable*: delayed-particle, proton, isomeric-transition and double-&beta; branches, which the ENSDF package does not cover, and exotic nuclides with no evaluated decay dataset.
- DICOM-coded nuclides: 96 of 101 links agree; the other 5 are branches of &le;0.1% that ICRP-107 omits.

29 reference pairs have no NUCLEON counterpart ([`daughter_comparison_reference_only.csv`](daughter_comparison_reference_only.csv)). They are all ENSDF pairs, mostly isomers whose ENSDF levels could not be matched one-to-one, plus small &alpha; branches (I-112, Cs-114).

**Branching ratios vs ICRP-107** ([`branching_vs_icrp107.csv`](branching_vs_icrp107.csv)): 1,575 of 1,595 mode-family comparisons agree within 1 percentage point. The 20 differences are on 11 nuclides with newer evaluations: V-50, Mn-58m, Y-91m, Nb-91m, In-112, Ir-190m2, At-219, U-228, Pu-232, Es-250 and Es-254m.

## 2. NUCLEON vs its IAEA source export

[`scripts/compare_to_source.py`](../scripts/compare_to_source.py) checks every row of [`source-data/IAEA nuclide livechart.csv`](../source-data) against the ontology ([`source_comparison.csv`](source_comparison.csv)): existence, stability, half-life in seconds, half-life value and unit as given, decay width, natural abundance, decay modes and branching ratios. After the v1.3 reload, 22 discrepancies remain, in 15 nuclides, all deliberate:

| Discrepancy | Nuclides | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flagged stable; export gives a very long half-life (7 also lack `:halfLife`) | Bi-209, W-180, Eu-151, Cd-116, Xe-134, Ba-132, Ce-138 | Primordial nuclides; stability flag kept pending a modelling decision |
| Flagged radioactive; export says stable | Sm-149, W-184 | As above |
| Decay modes | Hf-183, Mn-60 | The export's isomeric transitions moved to Hf-183m and Mn-60m (V3) |
| Decay modes | K-40 | NUCLEON: electron capture; export: EC + &beta;+ |
| Missing | n-1, n-4, n-6 | The neutron is not an element |

**Isomers are not in the export.** Their half-lives (716 isomers) still come from the earlier spreadsheet build; 6 differ from ICRP-107 by more than 5% (Nb-89m, Rh-102m, Sn-121m, Pr-134m, Ir-190m, Ir-190m2). The manual audit includes 15 isomers. The `:decayModeOrAbundance` free text also dates from that build and is kept verbatim.

## Corrections in v1.3

[`scripts/reload_from_source.py`](../scripts/reload_from_source.py) changed 1,347 nuclides; every change is listed in [`reload_from_source_log.csv`](reload_from_source_log.csv).

1. **Half-lives reloaded from the export** for 1,064 ground states, together with the value and unit as given and the limit notes (&gt;, &lt;, &ge;, ~). Examples: C-14 5730 &rarr; 5700 a; Se-79 1.13&times;10&#8310; &rarr; 3.27&times;10&#8309; a; Lu-177 6.734 &rarr; 6.6443 d.
2. **Na-24 unit error** fixed: 14.959 s &rarr; 14.956 h (53,842 s; ICRP-107 53,852 s).
3. **Np-242 / Np-242m** swapped back: 2.2 min / 5.5 min, agreeing with the export and ICRP-107.
4. **Natural abundance** added for the 287 naturally occurring nuclides (H-1 99.9855%, Fe-56 91.754%, U-235 0.7204%).
5. Decay widths reloaded for 31 resonances; half-life recomputed as &#8463; ln2 / &Gamma;. U-238 spontaneous-fission ratio 5.5&times;10&#8315;&#8309; &rarr; 5.45&times;10&#8315;&#8309;%.

The derived daughters do not depend on these fields, and the daughter comparison is unchanged.

## 3. Manual audit

[`scripts/draw_audit_sample.py`](../scripts/draw_audit_sample.py) assigns every nuclide to one stratum and draws a seeded random sample (seed 20261021):

| Stratum | Sampled | Pool |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| DICOM-coded radionuclides | 20 | 87 |
| Metastable isomers | 15 | 709 |
| Flagged stable | 10 | 249 |
| Z &ge; 89 | 10 | 466 |
| Half-life given as a limit or approximation | 5 | 152 |
| Half-life &lt; 1 s or given as a decay width | 10 | 757 |
| Two or more decay branches | 15 | 526 |
| One branch, half-life &ge; 1 s | 15 | 1,136 |
| **Total** | **100** | 4,082 |

**Protocol.** Two reviewers independently fill in the blank columns of [`audit_sample.csv`](audit_sample.csv) against the [IAEA LiveChart of Nuclides](https://nds.iaea.org/livechart/), each in their own copy. For each nuclide they record the IAEA half-life and decay modes, and mark half-life, modes and ratios, daughters, and abundance as Y or N. Discrepancies between reviewers are resolved by discussion. [`audit_sample_key.csv`](audit_sample_key.csv) lists which sampled nuclides the automated comparisons already flagged (3 of 100 after the v1.3 corrections). Keep it from reviewers until both have finished, so the audit stays blind; then report agreement with and without the key.

## Reproducing

```
pip install rdflib pandas radioactivedecay==0.6.1 paceENSDF==0.6.3
python3 scripts/reload_from_source.py NUCLEON.ttl --write   # already applied in v1.3
python3 scripts/add_display_annotations.py NUCLEON.ttl --write
python3 scripts/validate.py > validation/report.txt
python3 scripts/compare_daughters.py > validation/daughter_comparison_log.txt
python3 scripts/compare_to_source.py > validation/source_comparison_log.txt
python3 scripts/draw_audit_sample.py
```

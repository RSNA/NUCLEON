Before v1.3, half-lives, widths, spin-parities and free-text decay descriptions came from a 2017 spreadsheet of unknown provenance. Its half-lives matched NUBASE2003 (74.5% exact) and ICRP-107 (86% exact on ICRP's nuclides) better than any later evaluation, and included older values such as C-14 at 5730 a. None of its data remain in v1.4.

## 1. Systematic daughter comparison

**Method.** [`scripts/compare_daughters.py`](../scripts/compare_daughters.py) compares every NUCLEON branch that has a `:decayProduct` with a reference that names daughters explicitly:

- **ICRP Publication 107** (1,252 radionuclides), via the `radioactivedecay` 0.6.1 package (dataset `icrp107_ame2020_nubase2020`), for every parent it covers. All 1,252 are in NUCLEON.
- **ENSDF decay datasets**, via the `paceENSDF` 0.6.3 package (3,254 datasets), for parents not in ICRP-107. The package holds &alpha;, &beta;&minus; and EC/&beta;+ datasets only.

Links are compared by daughter nucleus (Z, A), since NUCLEON resolves every daughter to its ground state. ICRP-107's isomer labels are matched to NUCLEON's by half-life (e.g. ICRP "Lu-177m" is NUCLEON Lu-177m3, 160.4 d). ENSDF parent levels are matched to NUCLEON isomers by excitation energy, within 2 keV or 1%.

ICRP-107 is independent of NUCLEON's sources. The IAEA export draws on ENSDF, so ENSDF agreement mainly tests the derivation code.

**Results** for the 7,399 links with a daughter:

| Reference | Agree | Agree; reference daughter is an isomer | Branch not in reference | Disagree | Not checkable |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| ICRP-107 | 1,393 | 117 | 88 | 0 | &mdash; |
| ENSDF | 1,688 | &mdash; | 194 | 0 | 675 (mode not in ENSDF package) |
| Neither | &mdash; | &mdash; | &mdash; | &mdash; | 3,244 (parent in neither reference) |

- *Agree; reference daughter is an isomer*: e.g. Mo-99 &rarr; Tc-99m in ICRP-107, Tc-99 in NUCLEON, following NUCLEON's ground-state convention.
- *Branch not in reference, ICRP-107*: 48 of the 88 are branches of &le;1% and 33 have no branching ratio in the source; ICRP-107 omits both kinds. The rest reflect newer evaluations.
- *Branch not in reference, ENSDF*: mostly EC/&beta;+ branches of proton-rich nuclides for which the package has only an &alpha; dataset.
- *Not checkable*: delayed-particle, proton, isomeric-transition and double-&beta; branches, which the ENSDF package does not cover; exotic nuclides and short-lived isomers with no evaluated decay dataset.
- DICOM-coded nuclides: 96 of 101 links agree; the other 5 are branches of &le;0.1% that ICRP-107 omits.

Five reference pairs have no NUCLEON counterpart ([`daughter_comparison_reference_only.csv`](daughter_comparison_reference_only.csv)): &beta;&minus; branches of In-131m3 and Sb-129m2, and small &alpha; branches of I-112, Cs-114 and Pt-185m that the sources do not list.

**Half-lives and branching ratios vs ICRP-107** ([`halflife_vs_icrp107.csv`](halflife_vs_icrp107.csv), [`branching_vs_icrp107.csv`](branching_vs_icrp107.csv)). 44 of 1,252 half-lives differ by more than 5%: 35 ground states whose NUCLEON value matches the IAEA export exactly, and 9 isomers taken from NUBASE2020. In both cases the newer evaluation supersedes ICRP-107 (2008). 1,575 of 1,612 mode-family branching comparisons agree within 1 percentage point; the 37 differences are in 21 nuclides (15 isomers), likewise newer evaluations.

## 2. Ground states vs the IAEA source export

[`scripts/compare_to_source.py`](../scripts/compare_to_source.py) checks every row of the export against the ontology ([`source_comparison.csv`](source_comparison.csv)): existence, stability, half-life in seconds, half-life value and unit as given, decay width, natural abundance, decay modes and branching ratios. 6 discrepancies remain, all deliberate:

| Discrepancy | Nuclides | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Decay modes | Hf-183, Mn-60 | The export attributes the isomers' transitions to the ground states |
| Decay modes | K-40 | NUCLEON: electron capture; export: EC + &beta;+ |
| Missing | n-1, n-4, n-6 | The neutron is not an element |

## 3. Isomers from NUBASE2020

[`scripts/rebuild_isomers.py`](../scripts/rebuild_isomers.py) regenerates every isomer from NUBASE2020. An isomer is a NUBASE state with index 1&ndash;6 that has a half-life or is observationally stable: **2,019 isomers**. Isobaric analog states and levels with no half-life are excluded. Names follow NUBASE's state index: m, n, p, q, r, x &rarr; `m`, `m2` &hellip; `m6`, recorded as `:metastableState`. The excitation energy is a new property, `:excitationEnergy` (keV).

The 716 isomers of v1.3 were matched to NUBASE2020 states by half-life within each nucleus ([`isomer_mapping.csv`](isomer_mapping.csv)):

| Outcome | Isomers |
| --- | ---: |
| Same IRI | 626 |
| Renamed (NUBASE numbers the state differently) | 66 |
| Removed: no NUBASE2020 state (mostly actinide fission isomers, e.g. Pu-238m, Am-237m) | 24 |
| New in v1.4 | 1,329 |

64 IRIs from v1.3 still exist but now denote a different state, because NUBASE numbers states by excitation energy and lists short-lived states the 2017 spreadsheet omitted. Among them: **Lu-177m** (the 160.4-d isomer is now Lu-177m3), Sc-44m (58.6 h, now m3), Ag-110m (250 d, now m2) and Kr-83m (1.8 h, now m2). 27 matched isomers had 2017 half-lives more than 3&times; from NUBASE2020's. The 7 DICOM-coded isomers (Tc-99m, Kr-81m, In-113m, Sr-87m, Tc-94m, Mn-52m, Ir-191m) keep their IRIs, codes and SNOMED CT mappings.

## 4. Manual audit

[`scripts/draw_audit_sample.py`](../scripts/draw_audit_sample.py) assigns every nuclide to one stratum and draws a seeded random sample (seed 20261021):

| Stratum | Sampled | Pool |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| DICOM-coded radionuclides | 20 | 87 |
| Metastable isomers | 15 | 2,012 |
| Flagged stable | 10 | 244 |
| Z &ge; 89 | 10 | 466 |
| Half-life given as a limit or approximation | 5 | 156 |
| Half-life &lt; 1 s or given as a decay width | 10 | 757 |
| Two or more decay branches | 15 | 526 |
| One branch, half-life &ge; 1 s | 15 | 1,139 |
| **Total** | **100** | 5,387 |

**Protocol.** Two reviewers independently fill in the blank columns of [`audit_sample.csv`](audit_sample.csv), each in their own copy. Ground states are checked against the [IAEA LiveChart of Nuclides](https://nds.iaea.org/livechart/) and isomers against NUBASE2020. For each nuclide they record the reference half-life and decay modes, and mark half-life, modes and ratios, daughters, and abundance as Y or N. Discrepancies between reviewers are resolved by discussion. [`audit_sample_key.csv`](audit_sample_key.csv) lists which sampled nuclides the automated comparisons flagged (1 of 100). Keep it from reviewers until both have finished, so the audit stays blind.

## Reproducing

```
pip install rdflib pandas radioactivedecay==0.6.1 paceENSDF==0.6.3
python3 scripts/download_iaea.py                       # optional: refresh the IAEA export
python3 scripts/reload_from_source.py NUCLEON.ttl --write
python3 scripts/rebuild_isomers.py NUCLEON.ttl --write
python3 scripts/retire_legacy_fields.py NUCLEON.ttl --write
python3 scripts/add_display_annotations.py NUCLEON.ttl --write
python3 scripts/validate.py > validation/report.txt
python3 scripts/compare_daughters.py > validation/daughter_comparison_log.txt
python3 scripts/compare_to_source.py > validation/source_comparison_log.txt
python3 scripts/draw_audit_sample.py
```
